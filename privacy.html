Privacy

What we collect when you use this site or send us a listing, why we collect it, and what you can do about it.

Last updated 18 November 2026

Who we are

Ravenmoor Digital Ltd is the controller of your personal data. We are a company registered in England and Wales, company number [company number], registered office [registered office address].

For anything on this page, email hello@ravenmoor-digital.com.

When you send us the form

We collect what you type: your name, your agency or company, your email address, your phone number, the listing link or planning reference, and the style you chose. We also record the country the request came from.

We use it to make the sample or quote you asked for, to send it to you, and to call or email you about it. Our reason in law is that you asked us to do this before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

We keep an enquiry for 24 months, then delete it. If you become a customer, we keep the records we need for tax and accounts for as long as the law requires.

When you visit the site

Everyone: anonymous counts

We count how many visits the site gets, which sections are reached, which reels are opened, the kind of device, and the site you came from. These are totals only. They are not linked to you, and nothing is stored on your device to make them.

While a page is open we also keep a short-lived signal that someone is viewing a section, so we can see how many people are on the site. It is not stored on your device and it is deleted within minutes of you leaving.

You can switch all of this off on the Cookies page. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we send nothing.

If you press Accept: your visits, joined up

If you accept on the banner, we store a random ID in your browser. It lets us see what that browser did on this site: return visits, which reels you opened, how long you spent on prices, and whether you started the form. If you then send the form, we link that history to your enquiry.

Our reason in law is your consent. You can withdraw it at any time on the Cookies page, which also removes the ID. We delete this history after 90 days.

What we never collect

We do not store your IP address or your browser's user agent. We do not use advertising trackers, and we do not sell or share your data for marketing.

Who else handles your data

We use a small number of companies to run the site. Each one handles your data only to provide its service to us.

  • Cloudflare hosts the site and stores enquiries and site statistics.
  • Telegram delivers a private alert to us when an enquiry arrives. The alert contains the details you sent.
  • Google provides our email. Fonts on this site are also loaded from Google, which means your browser contacts Google's servers.
  • Our email delivery provider sends the confirmation you receive after sending the form.

Some of these companies process data outside the UK. Where they do, they rely on the legal safeguards they publish for international transfers.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to stop using it, or to move it elsewhere. You can object to how we use it, and you can withdraw consent you have given. Email hello@ravenmoor-digital.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If we change what we collect or why, we will update this page and the date at the top.
