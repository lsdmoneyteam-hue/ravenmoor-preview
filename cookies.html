What we store in your browser

This site sets no cookies. It uses your browser's local storage for three things.

NameWhat it is forWhen it is set
rd-cookieRemembers whether you pressed Accept or Reject, so the banner does not come back.When you answer the banner
rd-vidA random ID that lets us join up your visits and see which reels you watched.Only if you press Accept
rd-statsRemembers that you turned statistics off.Only if you turn statistics off

What we count without asking

We count visits, the sections people reach, the reels they open, the kind of device and the site they came from. These are totals only. They are not linked to you and nothing is stored on your device to make them. While a page is open we also know that someone is viewing a section; that is not stored on your device and is deleted within minutes.

UK rules allow this kind of anonymous counting without consent, as long as you can switch it off. The button at the top of this page does that. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, we already send nothing.

What changes if you accept

We store the random ID above and record what your browser does on this site: return visits, reels opened, time on prices, and whether you started the form. If you send the form, that history is linked to your enquiry. We delete it after 90 days. Pressing Reject on this page removes the ID straight away.

More detail is on the Privacy page.
